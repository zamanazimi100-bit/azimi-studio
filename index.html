⚠ POSSIBLE ACCOUNT OR DEVICE COMPROMISE? FOLLOW THE SAFE ORDER
  1. Use a trusted device if possible.
  2. Change the affected account password through the official provider.
  3. Enable or strengthen two-factor authentication.
  4. Review active sessions and remove unknown devices.
  5. Check recovery email and phone information.
  6. Check connected apps and revoke anything suspicious.
  7. Scan the device and install pending security updates.
  8. Contact the provider if you cannot recover the account normally.
